ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Shaping technology. Building tomorrow.

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata, focuses on developing strong foundations in computer science, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

The B.Tech programme in CSE (AIML) combines core computer science with machine learning, deep learning, data science, natural language processing and intelligent systems.

Through hands-on laboratories, technical workshops, hackathons, expert lectures and project-based learning, students are encouraged to transform classroom knowledge into practical solutions.

The department promotes innovation, research, entrepreneurship and collaborative learning while providing opportunities to interact with industry professionals, academics and fellow innovators.
